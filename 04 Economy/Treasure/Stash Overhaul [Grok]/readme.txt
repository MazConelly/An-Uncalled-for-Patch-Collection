-Explanation:

Small tweaks to default values:

-Helmets, tourniquets, sharpening stones, gun oil and glue have been added to the pool of possible items
-Weapons were listed into the pool of possible items both alone and with sights attached. The entries with sights have been removed to simplify the list and not overpopulate the world with scopes.
-Exos of all kinds had their progression tier increased.
-LMGs now have some progression to how they're found in stashes.
-Devices now have some progression to how they're found in stashes.
-Changes the many scientific stimpacks in the ISG stash bonus changes for some medical packs and artefact containers.

More tweaking may happens. Maybe not. Who knows.
